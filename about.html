About 1848

1848 Blooms & Designs creates florals for weddings and events, and teaches hands-on floral experiences.

How we design

Thoughtful design

Inspired by the beauty of a garden, we embrace the way flowers naturally grow and bloom. Layers of color, texture, movement and contrast come together to create arrangements that feel organic, romantic and effortlessly beautiful.

Intentional details

Every stem has its place. From varying heights and delicate blooms to unexpected textures and subtle color transitions, each detail is thoughtfully considered to create balance, depth and a natural sense of movement.

Unforgettable moments

Flowers have a way of transforming a space and how it makes us feel. We create floral designs that invite you to pause, take in the beauty and remember the moments that matter most.
